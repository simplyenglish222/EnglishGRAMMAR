Read about Vera Wang and Akio Morita. Complete the table.

Vera Wang

Vera Wang was born in New York in 1949 and her parents were from China. When she was younger she was a very good ice skater and she wanted to skate in the Olympics. However, she was very unhappy when she wasn’t in the US Olympic team in 1968 and she decided to become a fashion designer. She worked fifteen years for Vogue magazine, then another two years with Ralph Lauren. Today, she is a very successful designer. She has her own fashion label Vera Wang and sells dresses for $25,000!

Akio Morita

Akio Morita was born in 1921 and he was a businessman from the age of 25. He was the founder of Sony with his business partner and friend, Masaru Ibuka. At first they weren’t very successful. Their first product was an electric rice cooker and they only sold 100 of them because it always burnt the rice! Today, Sony is in the top 100 companies in the world.
